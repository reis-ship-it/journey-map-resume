Current direction

The strongest long-term projects in the portfolio are hybrid by design.

Motel Sunshine

An episodic 6DoF VR experience designed as a location-based story world with multiple character perspectives. It sits at the intersection of writing, immersive cinema, and emerging production technology.

Family Tree

A memorial-driven concept using volumetric capture, AI-assisted reconstruction, and time-based re-recording to explore memory, aging, and family history.

Creative systems

I also develop tools and experiments when the available workflow does not support the project. That technical layer is part of the producing and directing process, not separate from it. avrai.org is the clearest current example of that approach in public.
