Narrative world / Episodic XR concept

Motel Sunshine

Motel Sunshine is an episodic 6DoF VR experience anchored to a fictional motel and designed around revisitable perspective, character-centered routes, and an ending logic where leaving the world is part of the meaning.

The project sits between writing, immersive cinema, and future production tooling.

Novel concept / Adaptation path

Home Struck

Home Struck follows a child searching for belonging by moving through the world before fully understanding where home is. Written as a second-person novel, it is designed to create a direct relationship between reader, character, and eventual immersive adaptation.

In Development
Stage
Writing and adaptation development
Focus
Voice, belonging, reader proximity, cross-medium adaptation
Why now
Extends the narrative side of the portfolio into a longer-form emotional arc
Development considerations

The writing remains active, and the immersive version depends on the right production model rather than a rushed translation.

Volumetric memory system

Family Tree

Family Tree is a living memorial concept that combines volumetric capture, motion and voice analysis, and repeat scans over time to explore memory, aging, and continuity across years.

In Development
Stage

Focus
Memory, continuity, family history, immersive records
Why now
Shows the emotional and systems-thinking ambition behind the longer-range work
Development considerations

Scope, fidelity, safety, and infrastructure all matter here, so the concept needs to mature carefully.
